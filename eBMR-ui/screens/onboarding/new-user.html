What signing actually means here

When you sign a step, the system records your name, the exact time, the step you signed, and the meaning of the signature — performed, verified or approved. That record cannot be edited or deleted by anyone, including an administrator. It is the electronic equivalent of your handwritten initials on a paper batch record, and it carries the same legal weight.

You will be asked for this PIN dozens of times a shift. That friction is the point — it makes signing a conscious act rather than a click.

Three rules that will save you time

  • Record as you go, not afterwards. The system time-stamps when you type, and a gap between doing and recording is a finding in an audit.
  • If something is wrong, do not work around it. Raise the exception — that is a normal, expected part of the job, not a mark against you.
  • If the system blocks you, read the reason. It always says what is wrong and who can resolve it.
