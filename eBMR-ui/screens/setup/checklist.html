Get your organisation ready

Work through these seven steps once. Everything downstream — recipes, batches, execution, release — is built on the data you enter here.

3 of 7 complete

You can use the system before finishing all seven — but a batch cannot be issued until products, materials, BOM and equipment exist, because the recipe has nothing to point at.

Seven setup steps
Company & sitesLegal entity, sites, areas and lines UsersInvite the people who will use the system Roles & permissionsDecide what each role may do 4 ProductsWhat you manufacture 5 Materials & itemsRaw materials, components, packaging 6 Bill of materialsWhich materials go into each product 7 Equipment & roomsMachines, instruments and their calibration

Why this order?

Each step depends on the one before it. Users need sites to belong to. A BOM needs products and materials to exist before it can link them. A recipe needs a BOM and equipment before it can reference them. Doing it out of order means going back and filling gaps later.
