Why equipment lives in setup, not in the recipe

A recipe says "use a balance accurate to 0.001 kg". It does not name SC-4402. The specific instrument is chosen at execution time and recorded then — so when SC-4402 goes out for calibration, every recipe keeps working, and the record still shows exactly which balance weighed that batch.
